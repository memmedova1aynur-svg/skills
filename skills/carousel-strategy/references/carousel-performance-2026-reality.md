# Carousel performance: what the data does and doesn't say

> Volatile figures: **verify-quarterly**. Before citing any number, open the original source
> and check its date, sample and metric. Old articles' technical limits are not current rules.

## What large studies compare

Public benchmark studies compare **formats** (carousel vs Reels vs single image), not
**content types** (storytelling vs checklist vs case study). There is no verified, universal
ranking of content types by views. Don't claim one.

- **Socialinsider, 2026 Instagram organic benchmarks.** Reports that carousels drive the most
  saves and views across brand sizes and that carousel engagement held up better year over
  year than other formats, while overall Instagram engagement fell. (Sample size and exact
  figures: read them from the report itself before quoting; verify-quarterly.)
  https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram
- **Buffer, analysis of 4M+ posts published through Buffer, January 2022 – October 2024.**
  Reels led on reach; carousels led on engagement. Specific percentage gaps circulate (e.g.
  "+36% reach for Reels, +12% engagement for carousels"); confirm them in the original article
  before using them. https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/

These studies use different periods, samples (Socialinsider: brand accounts; Buffer: posts
scheduled through Buffer) and metrics. They describe averages, not a guarantee for any one page.

## Working hypotheses (to test per page, not a ranking)

- Views and shares: familiar problems, strong stories, before–after, argued expert positions.
- Saves: step-by-step, checklists, templates, resource collections.
- Discussion: scenarios, choices, comparisons, expert positions.
- Trust: case studies, annotated material, process.
- Applications: programme presentations, real results, Q&A.

## How to learn what works for a page

Post a type at least three times before judging it. Compare like with like (same goal, similar
topic weight). Read saves, shares and profile visits, not just likes: WoopSocial post analytics
store lifetime metrics for Instagram; use the native dashboard for anything it doesn't store
(`analytics-and-reporting`). Never fabricate a number.
