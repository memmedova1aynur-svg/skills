# Occasion content: 2026 reality

> Dates of movable holidays and awareness-day themes change each year: **verify-quarterly**,
> and verify every movable date in the year of posting. Never quote casualty figures,
> statistics or historical claims without an official source.

## Remembrance days (Azerbaijan)

20 Yanvar (Ümumxalq Hüzn Günü), 26 Fevral (Xocalı), 31 Mart (Azərbaycanlıların soyqırımı
günü), 27 Sentyabr (Anım günü).

- Brands that normally sell go quiet: one remembrance post or none, and **pause scheduled
  promotions** for the day.
- Visual language: dark or white background, a red carnation (qərənfil), a candle, the flag.
  No confetti, no bright accent slides, no emoji, no humour.
- **No realistic AI images of the events or the victims.** Symbolic imagery only.
- Copy: short, respectful, no "save / follow / link" asks, no hashtag stuffing.
- 27 September is also World Tourism Day internationally; in Azerbaijan, remembrance comes
  first and tourism posts move to another day.

## Religious holidays

Ramazan and Qurban bayramı follow the Hijri calendar and move about 11 days earlier each year;
the date is announced by the Caucasus Muslims Board. Calm greetings, family, sharing. Avoid
memes, sales-first posts, slaughter imagery on Qurban, and sacred symbols used as decoration.

## Novruz

- Azerbaijani Novruz imagery: **səməni** tied with a red ribbon, a **xonça** with
  **şəkərbura, paxlava, qoğal**, nuts and dried fruit, dyed eggs, candles, the **tonqal**.
- Not the Persian **haft-sin** table (seven items starting with "s", goldfish bowl). AI image
  tools default to it; prompts must say so.
- The four **çərşənbələr** (Su, Od, Yel, Torpaq) fall on the Tuesdays before Novruz: four
  natural carousel or story beats.
- Novruz can share 21 March with World Down Syndrome Day: two separate posts.

## Child development and disability days

- **Autism (2 April, and April as acceptance month):** the puzzle piece is widely rejected by
  autistic self-advocates; use the rainbow or gold infinity symbol. Avoid "suffers from",
  "cure", "recovery". Many autistic adults prefer identity-first language ("autist uşaq");
  many parents and clinicians use person-first ("autizmli uşaq"). Follow the client's
  practice and be consistent.
- **Down syndrome (21 March):** odd socks (#LotsOfSocks) is the recognised visual; avoid
  "illness" framing and pity or hero framing.
- **Disability day (3 December):** avoid "şikəst", "xəstə"; focus on access and inclusion.
- **Children's photos:** parental consent always; no identifiable children in AI-generated
  images presented as real.
- **Health claims** (hearing, speech milestones, breastfeeding): cite WHO or a national
  health authority; label milestone tables as general guidance, not diagnosis.

## Celebrations

New Year, 1 June, 8 March, national days: full brand palette, confetti and balloons fit.
Offers are fine for brands that sell, as long as the greeting comes first. The flag is drawn
correctly (1:2, crescent and eight-pointed star) and never carries a logo.

## Images: what audiences notice

- Stock "holiday" photos from another culture.
- The same AI image style every brand used that morning.
- Text baked into AI images with spelling mistakes in Azerbaijani letters (ə, ğ, ı). Keep all
  text in the renderer, never in the generated image.
